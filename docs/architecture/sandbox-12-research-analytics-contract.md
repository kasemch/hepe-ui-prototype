# HEPE SANDBOX-12Q/R — Research & Analytics Contract

Status: NON-PRODUCTION / ARCHITECTURE BASELINE

## Research Command (M08)
HEPE Research Command is organisational oversight, not a replacement for AWOS personal research workspace.

Lifecycle:
IDEA → CONCEPT → PROPOSAL → ETHICS → DATA → ANALYSIS → MANUSCRIPT → SUBMISSION → REVISION → PUBLICATION

HEPE may receive approved metadata, status and output references. Private AWOS notes, draft manuscripts and raw research data are not synchronised automatically.

## Analytics & Reports (M12)
Analytics is a derived/read-model layer only.

Candidate report families:
- Programme Health
- PLO/CLO Attainment
- Teaching Coverage
- Evidence Completeness
- QA Status
- Research Pipeline
- Publication Output
- Meeting Action Completion
- Course Request Status

Analytics must not mutate canonical records.

## Security
Research and analytics retrieval must reuse approved role/scope contracts. Public reporting must use explicit approved/public datasets only.
