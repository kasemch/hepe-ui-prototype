# Financial Literacy Learning Lab — Synthetic Boundary

Purpose: provide practice and reflection experiences without creating or implying authoritative programme completion criteria.

Permitted scenario families:
1. Needs vs wants classification.
2. Simulated monthly income/expense categorization.
3. Simulated budget construction and rebalance.
4. Simulated emergency-fund/savings goal planning.
5. Reflection on trade-offs after a synthetic financial shock.

Permitted event semantics:
- `finlit.activity_started`
- `finlit.activity_completed_synthetic`
- `finlit.requirement_unverified`

The word `completed` in the synthetic activity event means only that the learner finished that practice interaction. It does NOT mean Financial Literacy programme completion, readiness, certification, or satisfaction of an authoritative requirement.

Forbidden until an authoritative source is supplied and approved:
- official pass score;
- mandatory module count;
- official completion threshold;
- certificate eligibility;
- regulatory/professional readiness determination.

Suggested synthetic activity output fields: scenario identifier, selected choices, calculated simulated totals, reflectionSubmitted, attempt number, and timestamps. Do not require real bank balances, account numbers, debt identifiers, or other personal financial records for these learning scenarios.
